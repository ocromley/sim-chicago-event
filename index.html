About This Event

On behalf of the Society for Information Management (SIM) Chicago Chapter, we invite you to an exclusive evening designed for senior women IT executives and the next generation of women IT leaders.

"Join an intimate group of senior women IT executives and the next generation of women IT leaders at the Motorola Mobility Offices in the Merchandise Mart, for a private event featuring Lisa Davis."

Featured Speaker

Lisa Davis

Fortune 500 Executive • Keynote Speaker • Bestselling Author

Lisa Davis invites readers into the real story of what it takes to lead as a woman when the rules weren't written with you in mind—but you're determined to win anyway.

A tech leader across government, academia, and corporate industries, Lisa shares the battle-tested lessons she learned the hard way so you don't have to.
